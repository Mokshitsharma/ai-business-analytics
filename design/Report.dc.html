Executive summary

Roughly one customer in three is churning, and the pattern is consistent: short-tenure accounts on month-to-month contracts with recent support friction are leaving at more than twice the rate of the rest of the base. The model predicts churn with 87% accuracy, and contract type, tenure and support-ticket volume account for most of that signal.

Key insights
  • Month-to-month contracts carry ~2.4× the churn rate of annual contracts.
  • Churn drops sharply after 18 months of tenure.
  • 3+ support tickets in 30 days is a strong leading indicator.
Recommendations
  • Offer annual-contract incentives during onboarding.
  • Trigger a retention outreach after the 3rd support ticket.
  • Prioritise accounts under 6 months for success check-ins.
